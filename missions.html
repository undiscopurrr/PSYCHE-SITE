Sigma

Hammer of Consensus, Ender of Quarrels

Of contracts that speak with two tongues Sigma forges machine law, iron, beyond all argument. The logs lie scattered, each bearing its own account; one falls silent where another speaks, and some bear witness against others. Sigma seals them into a Proof of Reality Capsule (PoR Capsule), from which an auditor, an insurer, or any authorised third party may independently recompute and arrive where you arrived.

Cold is the hammer of consensus. Amid the clamour of the market it stands, ruled by reason alone. Sigma brings before you the truth of delivery no voice can gainsay, and keeps the sceptre of Acceptance in your hands.

From this hour, you buy neither a promise nor a figure settled six weeks too late, but certainty, firm as bedrock.

Enigma

Seer of the Undertow

When the world’s hunger for compute begins to drift, unheard, toward a region only now rising; when a class of hardware falters in silence; when a new corridor of compute trade first shows itself, a thin line on the dark water, Enigma has already risen from the mist above the unsounded deep of delivery data.

Before you, Captain, it lays the first signs by which you may change the course of events and carry the day.

The rest, dwindling in your rearview mirror, still seek the fading warmth of yesterday’s financial reports.
