Sigmahammer of consensus, ender of quarrels

Of contracts that speak with two tongues it forges machine law: iron, past all argument. The scattered logs, each side’s own account, each broken where the other’s is whole, it gathers and seals into one Proof of Reality Capsule, the PoR Capsule, so that an auditor, an underwriter, or any third party you empower may go apart into a room of their own, work the reckoning through again, and arrive where you arrived.

Cold is the hammer of consensus. Amid the clamor of the market it stands, ruled by reason alone, and spreads before you the truth of the delivery, a truth no voice can unsay; and into your hands it gives the sceptre of Acceptance.

From this hour you are buying neither a promise of compute, formless as mist, nor the grudging figure struck on a call six weeks too late, but certainty, set like bedrock.
