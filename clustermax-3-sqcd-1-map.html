ClusterMAX 3.0 mapped to SQCD-1

The record after a ClusterMAX test

ClusterMAX 3.0 tests and rates GPU clouds. SemiAnalysis’s SLA documents set three tiers and define acceptance testing, and SemiAnalysis encourages buyers, GPU clouds, lenders and insurers to use these terms “as a basis for their contracts.” SQCD-1 is an open format for compute delivery records, from which a party with no account on either side can recompute each party’s delivery figure for each window.

Fée-Fée Yang · PSYCHE · November 2026

This is a field map from the free text of SemiAnalysis’s ClusterMAX 3.0 article; the SLA documents and the paid closing section are outside it.

ClusterMAX 3.0 names the tests. SQCD-1 records who measured each reading and who signed its filing, each window’s review deadline and the date its quantity is final. A lender that reads both holds a rating of the GPU cloud and a delivery record whose figures it can recompute itself.
