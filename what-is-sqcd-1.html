Questions · SQCD-1

What is SQCD-1?

SQCD-1, Settlement Quality Compute Data, is PSYCHE's open standard for the record a delivered compute hour settles on. One record covers one delivery claim over one window, and two parties adopt the standard by naming its version and digest in their agreement.

Six field groupsSQCD-1 Parts I–II
  • A · Contract Standard. The clause, its metric and named function, its window, exclusions, thresholds, Grade Class and every field's verifiability tier sit under one digest filed before the window opens.
  • B · Measurements. Each series names its channel, samples, measured subject, instrument, clock source, aggregation and layer.
  • C · Missing Intervals. Every interval nobody observed is declared, attributed and valued.
  • D · Provenance. Each filing names its filing party, key and source class, records the independence of trust roots, and carries a signature over the filed set.
  • E · Temporal Binding. Each filing carries its signing time as claimed and an inclusion proof against a published log head.
  • F · Claim and Decision. The record holds the delivery claim, the signed decision of the buyer or its delegate under a grant, and the authority it was signed under, and the decision alone produces accepted.
Completeness§4.6

Give the record to someone who was absent and holds no account on either side. If the Period Figure, the shortfall and the amount owed all come out, and each traces to a named field, the record is complete; where a Divergence is carried to decision under Annex 2.7, each candidate figure and the amount it would owe come out, each with its basis.

Adoption§0

A contract adopts SQCD-1 by naming its version and the digest of its text: This Agreement incorporates SQCD-1, Version 1.1, identified by the digest published with it, as issued by PSYCHE, and no later version.

The text is published under CC BY-ND 4.0, so anyone may copy it, redistribute it and attach it to an agreement in full, unaltered and attributed.

The Filing Plan form, the record schemas and the conformance vectors are under the MIT licence, so an implementer may lift them into code.

Terms that decide who pays when the record is silent are commercial terms, and the parties fix them before the window, most of them by selecting a form from SQCD-1's Annex 2 and supplying its parameters.

Version 1.1 is dated 10 November 2026.
