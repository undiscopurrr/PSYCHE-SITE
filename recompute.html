Recompute the month yourself.

Open Annex 3’s worked month or Annex 9’s edge cases. Your browser checks the file against SQCD-1 and recomputes the worked month from the file and the standard alone: each Filing Party’s Period Figure under the declared function, the Divergence and the credit quoted on each filing.

SQCD-1 Full standard SQCD-1 Schemas & vectors

Arithmetic is exact: interval counts are integers, and every ratio is a fraction of integers. The page rounds the percentages it computes half up at the sixth decimal, the per-subject availabilities it compares at the eighth, and the dollar amounts at the cent. It shows each value from the file at its exact value, as a dollar amount, a percentage or a plain number. A row marked · carries no verdict, and the count above its list leaves it out.

Opened from disk, the page runs with the network off.

The vectors and MANIFEST.sha256 are published with SQCD-1 under the MIT licence. Where this page and SQCD-1 disagree, SQCD-1 governs.
