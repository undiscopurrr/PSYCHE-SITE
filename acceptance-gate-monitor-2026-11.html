Acceptance Gate Monitor · November 2026

No. 1.

WhiteFiber reports all 40 MW of contracted IT capacity at its NC-1 data center in operation. Initial funding of a $300 million term loan for NC-1, signed on November 9, is subject to "satisfaction or waiver of conditions relating to readiness and customer acceptance of the first 40 megawatts"; Nscale is the sole contracted tenant at NC-1. On November 10, WhiteFiber reported that no loans had been funded under the credit agreement.

A · Contract value behind a gateMap of 24 October; filings since

On the map, customer acceptance starts $27.6 billion of the $35.1 billion, and $27.1 billion of that sits in two Microsoft contracts.

"In New Jersey, we now expect to have delivered more than half of the capacity contracted with Microsoft by the end of the year, with the remaining tranches now scheduled for 2Q27."Nebius, letter to shareholders, 6-K Ex 99.2, 12 Nov 2026

IREN, in its November 5 quarterly report: at Microsoft's request, it is implementing a network design change across the Horizon deployments, and under the contract "delivery dates are extended, without delay credits, for delays resulting from a Microsoft-requested change." Discussions on delivery dates and on the application and payment of fees, credits and cost reimbursements run in parallel.

Anthropic's prospectus, filed November 19, puts up to about $85.9 billion with Nscale and Lambda on fees "accruing only as capacity is delivered to and accepted by us". Under the August agreement with Lambda, about $34.9 billion of Lambda's approximately $41.3 billion, either party could terminate if Lambda did not secure a facility lease by a specific date. Lambda did not, and the agreement "is currently terminable by either party". Neither party had delivered notice as of the prospectus date, and Anthropic said it does "not currently intend to terminate the agreement."

NVIDIA's neocloud commitments, $32 billion at their maximum, "take effect only if the neoclouds secure financing to purchase the data center infrastructure products and the related compute capacity is deployed, made operational, and accepted by us" (CFO commentary, November 17). In August the same line read $36 billion, and the commentary set no such condition.

C · Loans that wait on acceptanceTerms as filed or announced

IREN's Mackenzie financing, about $2.4 billion with its text filed on November 5, obliges no lender or noteholder to advance funds for any equipment until the agent receives a certificate of acceptance for that equipment executed by the borrower, an IREN subsidiary. The form reads: "DO NOT SIGN THIS CERTIFICATE OF ACCEPTANCE UNTIL BORROWER HAS RECEIVED AND INSPECTED THE EQUIPMENT."

On November 20, Apollo and Goldman Sachs announced the pricing of about $44 billion of notes to fund GPUs and related equipment for SpaceX. Lease schedules are expected to commence upon delivery, in the first half of 2027.
