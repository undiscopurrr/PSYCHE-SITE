Questions · GPU clouds and buyers

What happens when a GPU provider's uptime figure and the customer's differ?

Under SQCD-1, each party files its own measurements for every window, and either party computes the divergence between their figures from the filed record. Above the materiality threshold, a Divergence Event names the first interval at which the two series part.

Both sides fileSQCD-1 §B · §H

Provider telemetry and customer-side records arrive as separate submissions and stay separate.

Each party files for every window, whether or not it believes there is anything to dispute.

Unless the agreement names another minimum, the customer's series covers each measured subject's reachability, at an interval no coarser than the provider's, and its own record of work submitted, started and completed.

An interval is delivered only where every layer the obligation covers was conforming at the same time.

DivergenceSQCD-1 §H

At or below the materiality threshold fixed before the window, the record states concordance and names the figure that carries forward.

Above it, a Divergence Event carries both figures, the interval-level decomposition of each, the first interval at which the two series part, the layers and measured subjects at which they part, and each party's Unresolved Fraction.

A Divergence Event arises from the arithmetic alone.

Missing intervalsSQCD-1 §C · J1

An interval with no observation enters the record as a signed statement of its start and end, a coded cause, the party whose evidence is absent, the measured subject the interval is against, and its scope and materiality.

The Unresolved Fraction and the Unilateral Fraction stand on the face of the record.

Where a party's own Filing Plan required it to observe an interval and it neither observed nor declared it, the agreement's Profile decides, per obligation and by selecting one form from Annex 2.5, who bears that interval.

Reaching one quantitySQCD-1 Annex 2.7

The Profile selects the form that takes a material divergence to one quantity: both figures left standing for the parties to agree, a tolerance mean, a third reading with the two closest averaged, a referee, a panel, or a decision on a record that states both figures and the first interval at which they part.

Where the selected form needs a third party, that party is named before the window opens, with the basis on which it is paid.
