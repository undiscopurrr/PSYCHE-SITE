Settlement and Intelligence for contracted compute.

Settlement here is the settlement of delivery: the record of what was delivered, and what it earns under the contract, made final between the parties. Money moves afterwards, on rails outside PSYCHE, and consumes that record.

Who it is for

If you deliver compute

A customer says you did not deliver. You hand them a file.

Your month, filed under your own key against a standard both sides pinned before the window opened. The record travels with the invoice.

Engagements for providers →

If you buy it

The uptime figure you pay against is computed by the party that owes it to you.

The same evidence, recomputed on your own machine. The decision stays yours, and the quantity becomes something you can check.

Engagements for buyers →

If you lend against it, insure it, or audit it

The number you sign off on has one source today: the console of the party that owes it.

A record that holds in your hands without a login, a phone call, or anyone’s memory. Adopt it by reference, or read one with the party’s consent.

For lenders, insurers and auditors →

The turn

That month, one side took the other’s word.
The next one runs the other way.

Each side files under its own key, against a standard pinned before the window opened. Either side reaches the same result on its own machine.

How a month settles

01

Pinned

The standard is fixed before the window opens, and committed by digest.

02

Filed

Each party files its own measurements under its own key.

03

Recomputed

Six gates read what the evidence supports. The same bundle gives the same answer on any machine.

04

Signed

The party it binds decides. The record names the review that settled it.
