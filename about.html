Fée-Fée Yang

Founder, PSYCHE

Fée-Fée Yang reads markets through the unseen architecture of trust, settlement and systemic friction. For fifteen years, she has deployed her own capital in public equities, with an instinct for structural dislocations and a judgment that stands above market sentiment. In March 2020, amid the global liquidity shock, she acquired NVIDIA at approximately $6 a share, split-adjusted. In 2024, she entered Oklo below $6, near its historical trough, before exiting at roughly twenty-five times her entry price. She founded Isa Livermore Consulting in 2021 to conduct her investment work independently.

As billions flowed into artificial intelligence infrastructure, Yang examined the financial architecture taking shape beneath it: the intricate relationship between physical capacity, contractual authority and institutional capital. Her attention settled on the conditions governing delivery, acceptance and the financial obligations they set in motion.

Drawing on the settlement disciplines of physical electricity markets, Yang conceived PSYCHE in 2026, bringing their precision to cryptographic verification and contractual finality. She designed its governing architecture, established SQCD-1, and directed the development of the Sigma verification engine and the Proof of Reality Capsule framework.

Today, Yang directs PSYCHE’s architecture, protocol design and engineering, establishing the delivery verification infrastructure for the global compute economy.
