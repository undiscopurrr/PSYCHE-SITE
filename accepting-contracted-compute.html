Questions · GPU clouds and buyers

How is acceptance of contracted GPU compute recorded?

Under SQCD-1, the buyer answers the provider's delivery claim with a signed decision: accept, reject, dispute or abstain. Only a signed accept produces accepted, and the record names the authority it was signed under.

Acceptance as a fee triggerSEC filings

In PSYCHE's acceptance-gate map, six AI compute contracts filed with the SEC, worth about $35.1 billion in stated or maximum value, begin their recurring fees or rent on a buyer's acceptance of delivered hardware, a provider's ready notice or a lease's commencement.

Of that value, $27.6 billion starts on the buyer's own acceptance.

IREN's contract with Microsoft starts each tranche's service term and monthly fees on acceptance of its minimum GPU quantity, and its fees count accepted GPUs only.

The recordSQCD-1 §F · B8.5 · I.1.1
  • Claim. The provider's delivery claim states its window, standard, subject, the pass it rests on and the value at risk, with the quantity carried forward and its evidence set.
  • Grade. A commencement reading establishes that each of the contracted Grade Class's fields the buyer can measure itself was delivered, and later readings establish that those fields were still delivered at the time of each reading.
  • Decision. The buyer signs one of accept, reject, dispute or abstain, and a reject or a dispute is terminal at once.
  • Authority. The record names the grant the decision was signed under and the limits of that grant, and a signature given outside its grant binds no one.
  • Delegate. A delegate the buyer named decides inside a grant the buyer issued.
  • Silence. A buyer that files no decision by the review deadline leaves the Case expired, and expiry is priced under a form both parties chose before the window; the contract's own rule still decides acceptance.
Verification and acceptanceSigned in Advance

Verification asks whether a body of evidence supports a claim under a stated standard, and anyone holding the same inputs can recompute the answer. Acceptance asks whether the party the claim binds agrees to be bound, and only that party, or someone it authorized, can answer.

PSYCHE settles delivery claims for contracted compute on that separation: Sigma, PSYCHE's verification engine, returns a result, and only the buyer's signed decision, or its delegate's under a grant, produces accepted.
