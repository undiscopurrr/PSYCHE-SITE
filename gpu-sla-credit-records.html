What record does a GPU SLA credit claim need?

Under SQCD-1, an SLA credit turns on one figure per window, computed from samples under a function both parties named before the window opened. SQCD-1 records the function, both parties' samples and every interval nobody observed, so someone with no account on either side can recompute each party's figure.

Credits todaySQCD-1 §2 · Public SLAs

SQCD-1 §2 cites a practitioner guide to cloud service agreements, which describes the credit process as one in which the customer supplies proof of the outage and the CSP reviews the claims and makes a final, unilateral judgment on service credits.

AWS's DynamoDB and Compute SLAs pay credits on one account's own monthly figure. For a claimed credit, the customer computes that figure from its own request logs and AWS confirms it.

Both SLAs require request logs with a claim and set no method or cadence for keeping them.

Fixed before the windowSQCD-1 §1 · §A

The agreement sets its availability target, price and remedy schedule, and SQCD-1 requires each to be identified and fixed before the window opens.

  • A2 names the metric and the function that turns samples into a Period Figure.
  • A4 lists the exclusions, each with the evidence required to invoke it.
  • A5 states the shortfall that contributes nothing and the level at which the obligation is missed.
  • A7 references the remedy schedule that converts a shortfall into money.
  • A10 commits A1 through A9 under one digest, carried in an event that precedes the window.

Two parties averaging differently will produce different months from identical samples.

After the windowSQCD-1 §I.1 · H4

Days are business days after the window closes, unless the agreement states otherwise.

  • By W+2, each party files its measurements and declares its missing intervals.
  • By W+5, the divergence between the two parties' figures is computed.
  • By W+7, the delivery claim is filed.
  • By W+12, the buyer's decision is due.
  • At W+30, the correction window closes; the Case decided at W+12 stands, and the difference a correction establishes is claimed by the party it favors as a credit Claim under the agreement's credit terms, or recorded as an amendment both parties sign.
  • At W+45, the quantity is final, and at W+90, the money is final.

Where the parties' figures differ by more than the materiality threshold, a Divergence Event names the first interval at which their series part.
