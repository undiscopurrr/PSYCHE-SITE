Does silence count as acceptance in a GPU contract?

In four of the ten SEC-filed AI compute contracts in PSYCHE's acceptance-gate map, a buyer that neither accepts nor rejects within the acceptance window has accepted. Under SQCD-1, silence ends a Case as expired, and the cost of expiry is a term the parties fix before the delivery window; the contract's own rule still decides acceptance.

In the filed contractsSEC filings

The acceptance windows are 5 days for IREN, unit not stated; 7 business days for Cohere and Thinking Machines Lab; and redacted for Nebius.

Boost Run's contract with Cohere treats silence or production use as acceptance, and problems not listed in a rejection are waived.

A contract's rule deeming silence or production use to be acceptance applies whatever any later record says.

Silence under SQCD-1I.1.1 · Annex 2.4

Where no decision is filed by the review deadline, the Case resolves as expired, derived from the event that shows the deadline passed.

The agreement's Profile selects the silent party's cost of expiry from four forms:

  • EX-01: expiry closes the Case, and nothing is owed on the claim either way.
  • EX-02: the underlying invoice remains payable on its own terms, and the claim for remedy in this Case lapses unexercised.
  • EX-03: the amount computed under the remedy schedule is owed, uncontested.
  • EX-04: the silent party pays an amount fixed in advance and independent of the merits.

EX-02 and EX-03 are symmetric, and both are ordinary negotiated positions.

Each Case shows the source of its terminal state: on a delivery claim, accepted comes only from the buyer's signed decision, or its delegate's under a grant, and expired comes from the deadline passing.
