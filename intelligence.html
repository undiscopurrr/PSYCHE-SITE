PSYCHE Intelligence · Charter

Settled, then read.

Intelligence reads the record after the parties have signed it. Four articles govern every publication.

I

Two channels

Settlement is decided by the parties who sign it. Intelligence reads that record afterwards, on a channel of its own.

II

Coverage

A pattern is published when enough independent participants carry it. Below that line the publication reads insufficient coverage.

III

One version

Within a jurisdiction, every entitled reader receives the same version and the same measure at the same moment.

IV

Correction

Every publication carries its own path of correction and withdrawal.

Access is by entitlement.

Enigma is introduced in Missions.
