Questions · Lenders

How does a lender confirm that financed GPUs were delivered and accepted?

Under SQCD-1, PSYCHE's open standard for delivery records, a lender confirms delivery from a record both parties file. Each window's record carries terms fixed before the window, each party's signed measurements, the provider's delivery claim, and the buyer's signed decision or the Case's expiry. SQCD-1 counts a record complete when someone absent, with no account on either side, can recompute from it the Period Figure, the shortfall and the amount owed, each traced to a named field, and, where a Divergence is carried to decision, each candidate figure and the amount it would owe, each with its basis.

Acceptance in financing arrangementsSEC filings

In PSYCHE's acceptance-gate map, two SEC-filed financing arrangements reach into acceptance gates.

Under CoreWeave's $2.6B delayed-draw loan, a replacement or additional customer contract qualifies only if, among other criteria, the customer has accepted any additional GPU clusters under it.

IREN's financing of its Microsoft contract lowers the parent's minimum liquidity as Microsoft accepts each tranche, from $200M before the first acceptance to $0 on the fourth tranche's acceptance or termination.

A filing reports a passed gate as a status line, without the evidence behind it: IREN's annual report states that Microsoft accepted the first of four tranches in August 2026. No filing in PSYCHE's acceptance-gate map contains a ready-for-test notice, a validation report, a test result or an acceptance notice.

The recordSQCD-1
  • Terms. The clause, its metric and named function, its window, exclusions, thresholds and remedy schedule reference sit under one digest filed before the window opens.
  • Measurements. The provider and the buyer each file their own series for every window, each under its own key.
  • Grade. A Grade Class names the kind of compute hour an obligation is for, from accelerator to software floor. A commencement reading establishes that each of the contracted Grade Class's fields the buyer can measure itself was delivered.
  • Decision. Only the buyer's signed decision on the delivery claim, or its delegate's under a grant, produces accepted, and the record names the authority it was signed under.
  • Silence. A Case with no decision by its review deadline ends as expired, and the cost of expiry is a term the parties fixed before the window; the contract's own rule still decides acceptance.
  • Quarter. Each quarter, a named officer of each filing party affirms in writing that its Filing Plan was implemented and followed in every window of the quarter, save the windows the officer identifies as exceptions. A party that failed to file for more than the unresolved ceiling across a quarter's windows may give no attestation for that quarter.

SQCD-1 runs its covenant clock for whoever lent against the contract.

Reading under an access grantSQCD-1 §K

A filing party may give a lender access to the filed record under an access grant, and each access grant names its lender, windows and duration.

A record disclosed under an access grant carries its signatures and inclusion proofs, and the lender checks it on its own.

Once a lender relies, under an access grant, on a filing with a valid signature and an inclusion proof, the party that made the filing may not rely on a contrary fact against the lender, unless it shows the lender had notice of the filing's non-conformity at the time of reliance.
